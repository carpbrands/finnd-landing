Which One Should You Get?

Get the refill adapter if: You want to keep using your portable 1lb tanks (for backpacking, small stoves, etc.) but stop paying full price for them. Refill at home, take the small tanks with you.

Get the splitter if: You're car camping or have a semi-permanent setup where you can bring the 20lb tank with you. Skip the small tanks entirely and run everything directly.

Get both if: You do a mix of activities. Refill small tanks for backcountry trips, use the splitter for car camping weekends.

Safety Note: When refilling 1lb tanks, always do it outdoors, never overfill past 80% capacity, and check for leaks with soapy water before use. Store refilled tanks upright in a well-ventilated area.
